## Product Bet

Winning is treated as a retention design problem:

- The first minute must make the genre and conflict legible by ear.
- The episode engine must support dozens or hundreds of installments.
- Each ending should create "binge debt": an unresolved promise the listener wants paid.
- The prose layer matters, but the tool should first test hook, desire, status, payoff, and audio clarity.

## Mechanism Inventory

- Retention Black Box: predicts minute-level drop-off.
- Cliffhanger Futures Market: ranks ending options like volatile assets.
- Desire Lattice: turns character wants into a conflict graph.
- Binge Debt Ledger: tracks promises and payoff timing.
- Audio Mouthfeel Meter: checks whether lines survive voice performance.
- Trope Mutation Lab: keeps genre familiarity while warping one sacred trope.
- Cold Open Split Test: compares first-minute acquisition strategies.
- Serial DNA: creates a repeatable ritual, phrase, or sonic identity.
